Baking and Troubleshooting

Bake the loaf and address common problems.

Baking Your Sourdough Loaf

  • Milestone of your sourdough journey
  • Final step after feeding, mixing, fermenting
  • Proper technique prevents common mistakes

Preheat & Use Steam

  • Preheat Dutch oven lid on for 30+ mins
  • Use hot water tray for steam
  • Ensures rise and crisp crust

Score the Dough

  • Cut 1–2 shallow lines at 30–45°
  • Use sharp blade for clean cuts
  • Controls expansion and shape

Bake Covered & Uncovered

  • Covered: 20 minutes for steam rise
  • Uncovered: 20–30 minutes for crisp crust
  • Never bake uncovered from start

Check Doneness with Thermometer

  • Insert into center of loaf
  • Ideal temp: 200–210°F (93–99°C)
  • Too low = gummy; too high = dry

Fix Common Problems

  • Dense crumb? Extend fermentation
  • Poor rise? Check starter & proofing
  • Burnt crust? Lower temp or shorten time

Worked Example & Try It

  • Proper preheat, steam, and timing
  • 205°F = perfect doneness
  • Record results to improve next time
