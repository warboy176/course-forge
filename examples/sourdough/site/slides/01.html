What Is Sourdough and How to Start a Starter

Learn the basics of sourdough and begin creating a starter.

What Is Sourdough?

  • Natural leavening from wild yeast & bacteria
  • Made with just flour, water, and time
  • No commercial yeast needed
  • Tangy flavor from fermentation

What Is a Starter?

  • Living culture of flour & water
  • Captures wild microbes from air & grain
  • Ferments sugars to rise & flavor bread
  • Needs daily care to thrive

How to Mix Your Starter

  • Use 100g flour + 100g water
  • Stir until smooth in a clean jar
  • Cover loosely for airflow
  • Mark level with rubber band

Feeding Schedule (Days 1–5)

  • Feed every 12 hours
  • Discard half before each feeding
  • Add 100g flour + 100g water
  • Keep at room temperature

Signs of a Healthy Starter

  • Visible bubbles throughout
  • Rises 1–2 inches after feeding
  • Tangy, fruity, or wine-like smell
  • No foul odors like rotten eggs

Day 3 Example

  • 300g total volume after two feeds
  • Bubbles visible, rising 1.5 inches
  • Mild sour smell, not unpleasant
  • Ready for next feeding cycle

Try It Now!

  • Start with 100g flour + 100g water
  • Feed every 12 hours for 5 days
  • Discard half, then feed
  • Be patient—progress takes time

Recap & Key Tips

  • Starter = living culture of microbes
  • Feed daily, discard half each time
  • Look for bubbles, rise, tangy smell
  • Keep at 70–75°F for best results
