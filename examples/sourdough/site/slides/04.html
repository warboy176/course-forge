Shaping and Proofing the Loaf

Shape the dough and prepare it for baking.

Shaping & Proofing: Key to Great Sourdough

  • Transforms dough into structured loaf
  • Holds gas for even rise and crumb
  • Critical for flavor, texture, and oven spring
  • Poor shaping = collapsed or uneven bake

Palm Press Method: Create Tension

  • Turn dough out on lightly floured surface
  • Press with palm to form oval shape
  • Fold edges toward center, tuck under
  • Rotate and repeat for tight, smooth surface

Proofing: Support & Rest

  • Use banneton or floured bowl
  • Dust with rice flour to prevent sticking
  • Seam-side up for best shape retention
  • Cover to prevent drying and skin formation

Proofing Options: Time & Temperature

  • Room temp: 2–4 hours, open crumb
  • Cold proof: 8–12 hours, deeper flavor
  • Retardation slows fermentation
  • Let cold dough rest 1–2 hours before baking

Check Readiness: The Poke Test

  • Gently press side of dough
  • Slow spring-back (2–3 sec) = ready
  • Fast return = under-proofed
  • No return = over-proofed

Worked Example: 12-Hour Cold Proof

  • Shape at 6:00 PM, place in fridge at 6:30
  • Let sit at room temp 1.5 hours next day
  • Test: slow spring-back = perfect
  • Score and bake for best results

Try It: Step-by-Step

  • After bulk fermentation, shape with palm press
  • Place seam-side up in banneton or bowl
  • Cover and proof—room temp or fridge
  • Test with poke test before baking
