Maintaining and Feeding Your Starter

Keep your starter active and ready for baking.

Why Your Starter Needs Care

  • A living culture needs regular feeding
  • Proper care ensures reliable bread
  • Healthy starter = better rise & flavor
  • Consistent feeding keeps it active

Feed Correctly: The 1:1 Rule

  • Use equal weight: 100g flour + 100g water
  • Avoid volume measurements (cup sizes vary)
  • Use unbleached bread or whole wheat flour
  • Filter or boil tap water to remove chlorine

Storage Based on Baking Schedule

  • Room temp: Feed every 12–24 hours
  • Fridge: Feed once weekly
  • Cold slows fermentation but keeps it safe
  • Warm up 2–3 hours before baking

When Is Your Starter Ready?

  • Rises in 4–6 hours after feeding
  • Doubles in size with bubbles
  • Smells tangy, fruity—not rotten
  • No alcohol or hooch smell

Fix Common Problems

  • No rise? Too cold or underfed
  • Alcohol smell? Feed more often
  • Hooch? Pour off and feed
  • Mold? Discard and restart

Worked Example: Bake on Saturday

  • Friday night: Take from fridge, feed
  • Saturday morning: Should be bubbly and rising
  • Use 100g in dough, then feed again
  • Return to fridge until next bake

Try It Now

  • Weigh 100g starter, discard extra
  • Add 100g flour + 100g water
  • Mix, cover, check every 3 hours
  • Track bubbles and rise time

Key Takeaways

  • Feed by weight, not volume
  • Store by baking frequency
  • Ready in 4–6 hours, double in size
  • Smell sour, not rotten or alcoholic
