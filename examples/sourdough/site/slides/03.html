Mixing and Bulk Fermentation

Prepare dough and manage the first fermentation phase.

Why Weight Matters

  • Use a scale for precision
  • Volume varies—weight doesn’t
  • Consistency in every bake
  • Start with grams, not cups

Basic Sourdough Recipe

  • 500g bread flour
  • 500g water (70°F)
  • 100g active starter
  • 10g fine sea salt

Mixing & Autolyse

  • Combine flour and water
  • Stir until no dry bits remain
  • Rest 30 minutes (autolyse)
  • Let gluten start forming naturally

Stretch & Fold Technique

  • Wet hands to prevent sticking
  • Stretch one side, fold over
  • Rotate bowl 90°, repeat
  • Do 4–6 sets over 2–3 hours

What Ready Dough Looks Like

  • Puffy and slightly jiggly
  • Full of visible bubbles
  • Holds shape when pressed
  • Soft, elastic, not sticky

Timing & Environment

  • 3–4 hours at 70–75°F
  • Warmer = faster fermentation
  • Cooler = slower, longer rise
  • Check dough, not just time

Try It Now

  • Weigh ingredients accurately
  • Autolyse 30 min
  • Stretch and fold 4 times
  • Check for puffiness & bubbles
