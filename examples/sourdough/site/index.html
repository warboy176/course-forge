Questions

Do I need special equipment to start?

No. You only need a jar, flour, and water. Everything else is basic kitchen gear. We’ll walk you through what you need step by step.

How long does it take to get a working starter?

Most people see signs of activity in 5 to 7 days, but it can take up to 10. The key is consistent feeding and patience—no rush.

What if my starter doesn’t rise or smells bad?

A sour or slightly funky smell is normal. If it’s slimy or has mold, discard it and start over. Most issues come from inconsistent feeding or temperature.

Can I make sourdough if I’m short on time?

Yes. We cover flexible schedules and simple timing adjustments. Even with a busy life, you can maintain a starter and bake every few weeks.
